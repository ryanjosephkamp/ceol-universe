## The English Wiktionary

The fields `wiktionary_english`, `wikt_title_screen`, `wikt_title_any`, `wk_proper_noun`, and `wk_flags`, and the listing of the spaced rows, are derived from the English Wiktionary's pages-articles dump and all-titles list of the run of August 1, 2026 (https://dumps.wikimedia.org/enwiktionary/). The text of Wiktionary is written by its contributors and is available under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike License (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) and the GNU Free Documentation License, under the Wikimedia Foundation's Terms of Use (https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Terms_of_Use). Each page's history lists its authors (https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Special:History). The fields here are derived from the pages and are not their text; they were modified by extraction under the CEOL grammar.

The channel label `eol:dictionary-api:free-dictionary` records that the Free Dictionary API (https://dictionaryapi.dev/), which draws its entries from the English Wiktionary under the same license, returned an entry for the string to the English OpenList.

## The Google Books Ngram corpus

The `gb_` fields of the features config, `zipf_log10_rank`, `zipf_resid`, and `gb_type` are aggregates per string of the Google Books Ngram corpus, English 1-grams, version 20200217 (googlebooks-eng-20200217; https://storage.googleapis.com/books/ngrams/books/datasetsv3.html). The export page states: "This compilation is licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported License" (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). The values are derived from it. The corpus is described by Jean-Baptiste Michel and colleagues, "Quantitative Analysis of Culture Using Millions of Digitized Books", Science 331 (2011).
